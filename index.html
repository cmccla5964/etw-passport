A Chicago food passport

I'm making a printed passport with one Chicago restaurant for each country.

Vietnamese on Argyle. Pakistani on Devon. Polish in Avondale. Chicago already has most of the world in it.

The passport is a small book. Each page is a country, and each country is one independent Chicago restaurant: who runs it, what to order, where it is, and an empty space for a stamp.

You bring the book in, eat, and the restaurant stamps your page and gives you a small thank-you on your first visit. That's the whole idea.

The first edition is planned for this holiday season. I'm reaching out to restaurants one at a time, so if you got an email from me, this is what it was about. The details for restaurants are just below.
