Cross Streets: A Passport for Chicago Restaurants

As much as I love traveling and exploring new cultures, I’m often guilty of leaving my own backyard unexplored. Crossing the street to try Uzbekistani food, at first, feels much less enticing than crossing the world to visit Uzbekistan. The prospect of a new place, new people, and new food are simply more exciting when they form part of an adventure.

I’m bringing the Cross Streets passport to life because I want to create that same sense of adventure and possibility within my own city. I want to give myself and others an excuse to be curious in our own backyard.

The passport will look and feel like a real one. There is simply something tantalizing about an unmarked passport page and I want the booklet to inspire Chicagoans to explore our city's restaurants as if they’re traveling the world. Each featured country will get a page about one independent restaurant and its story, along with what to order and where to find it. When a passport holder eats there, the restaurant stamps their page, and on their first visit they get a free appetizer or dessert with their entrée.

I'm choosing first-edition restaurants by December 4 and launching at the start of 2027. If you’re interested in having your restaurant featured or getting on the waitlist for a passport, just drop your email in one of the forms below.
